PARTY PORTAL 3.4.1 - HOW TO INSTALL (27 November 2026)

Two files, on top of Party Portal 3.4 (upload 3.4 first if it is not on the site yet):
  channels.json  - the playlists: rock 166 -> 383 videos, bikini runway 150 -> 241, fashion 102 -> 119
  CHANGELOG.md   - the 3.4.1 entry on top

1. Open the PartyPortal repository on github.com.
2. "Add file" -> "Upload files", drag in channels.json and CHANGELOG.md (they replace the old ones).
3. "Commit changes".

Nothing else is needed: the page fetches channels.json network-first, so visitors get the new playlists on their
next visit - no cache change in sw.js. The site's daily workflow keeps checking every video (curate.py) as before.
