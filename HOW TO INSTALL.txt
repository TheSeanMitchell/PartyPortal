WHAT CHANGED
  1. The page: four big screens first (a 2x2 block filling the width the 10-tile wall used),
     then a TOP 10 MOST REPORTED (two columns of five, like NUZU's), then On The Horizon, then
     the news. With LIVE VIDEO off the headlines lead. Phones keep the single player.
  2. The top bar mirrors NUZU's: SAVED, UPDATED, LIGHT, LIVE VIDEO, PARTY MODE, SHUFFLE.
     LIVE VIDEO (key V) is remembered; it is ON until someone turns it off.
  3. Each screen has a next-video button and a new-channel button; one screen always comes from
     the core lanes; every visit deals a different set (10 visits: 40 videos, no repeats).
  4. News like NUZU's: about 250 feeds (was 145), the same story grouped across outlets and
     counted. On the same news: stories reported by 2+ outlets 22 -> 91. The fire now means
     "several outlets, under 24 hours" (it was on every story). Shopping posts, ticket how-tos,
     horoscopes, sports and TV, and copy-paste sites are filtered out; ticket news stays.
  5. More to watch: swim runway 60 -> 73 videos, fashion shows 74 -> 148, live rock 81 -> 166.
     Official uploads only, each new video checked playing on the live site.
  6. The winter calendar: 29 new dates from official pages - New Year's Eve in 13 cities,
     the Australian and New Zealand summer festivals, Thailand, Tulum - and a New Year's Eve
     countdown with a button for the world's NYE parties. 52 festivals in all (was 23).
  7. While the page is hidden (another browser tab or another Argus tab) the silent screens
     pause; the one being heard keeps playing.
  8. health.json each build (https://theseanmitchell.github.io/PartyPortal/health.json).

HOW TO UPLOAD (about three minutes)
  1. Open https://github.com/TheSeanMitchell/PartyPortal
  2. Add file > Upload files. Drag in index.html, channels.json, bot.py, CHANGELOG.md,
     README.md and sw.js from this folder. Commit message, for example: "v3.2". Commit changes.
  3. Still on GitHub, open the folder .github, then workflows. Add file > Upload files. Drag in
     daily-content.yml from this folder's .github\workflows folder (it replaces the old one).
     Commit changes.
  4. The upload of bot.py starts the refresh by itself (Actions tab: "Daily Culture Feed
     Refresh"). If it does not appear within a minute: Actions > Daily Culture Feed Refresh >
     Run workflow.

CHECK (about five minutes after step 4)
  - https://theseanmitchell.github.io/PartyPortal/ (reload once if the old page shows): four
    big screens, then TOP 10 MOST REPORTED, then On The Horizon with the New Year's countdown.
  - The top bar shows the LIGHT and LIVE VIDEO switches; LIVE VIDEO off stops every screen.
  - https://theseanmitchell.github.io/PartyPortal/health.json shows "version": "3.2.0".
